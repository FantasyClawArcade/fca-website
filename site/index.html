How it works

  1. Pick your machine

    Walk the rows and find the prize you want, from mini claws to full-size digital cranes.

  2. Line up your shot

    Steer the claw into position, drop it and go for the prize.

  3. Take home your prizes

    Every prize you grab is yours to keep: trending plush, anime and kawaii goods, toys and novelty, premium jumbo prizes and claw exclusives.

We bring in new prizes regularly, so there's always something new to go for.

Fantasy Claw Arcade

Experience our unique blend of entertainment and gaming at Fantasy Claw Arcade, a growing claw arcade franchise in the United States. What started as the largest claw arcade in Las Vegas, NV, has grown into a nationally expanding claw arcade franchise with locations across the country. We combine the thrill of gaming with the joy of winning prizes, offering a unique skill-based claw crane experience inspired by Japanese culture. Unlike traditional arcades, every game we feature puts the prize right in front of you.

Our prize selection is geared towards audiences of all ages, because it's not just kids that love playing claw arcades! From plush of all sizes to nostalgic collectibles to cutting-edge gadgets, there's something for everyone. With a wide range of claw and prize redemption machines, we promise an unforgettable experience.
