Birthdays & Events

Celebrate With Us!

Looking for a birthday party or group event that’s as fun as it is unforgettable? At Fantasy Claw Arcade, we make celebrations magical from the very first token drop.

What’s Included

Tokens for Every Guest

Each party package comes with a preset number of tokens per guest, so everyone gets in on the action right away.

Tables & Setup

At Fashion Show Mall and AREA15, we’ll set up tables so you have space to celebrate, serve food, and keep the party flowing.

Food & Drinks

Skip the hassle. At Fashion Show Mall and AREA15, we can help coordinate food and beverage orders so you can focus on having fun.

Fun & Prizes

Always the best part. You and your guests will have an amazing, memorable time enjoying the arcade.

Miracle Mile Shops, International Market Place and Easton host events too. There’s no room for tables and chairs there, so events are all about the games.

Go Big: Rent the Entire Arcade

Want the whole place to yourself? Full-arcade rentals are available for birthdays, corporate events, and private parties. Contact us to customize your package and make it an exclusive experience.
