What to expect

80+ prize machines inside AREA15, Las Vegas's immersive entertainment destination. Line up the claw, time your drop and go for plush, collectibles and high-value prizes.

Stop by the trade-up counter for collectible upgrades. It's a fun stop for families, date nights and groups.

Finding us

We're on the first floor of the main AREA15 building, at the far end past Omega Mart.

Parking is free in the AREA15 main lot.

Parties and groups

Birthday parties, group outings and private events are available here, with room for tables, chairs, food and drinks. You can also rent out the whole arcade for a private party.
