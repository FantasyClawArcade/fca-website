Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Fantasy Claw Arcade, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, www.fantasyclawarcade.com. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect personally identifiable information (PII), such as:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you voluntarily provide it to us through forms on our website, such as when you contact us, request information about a franchise, plan a birthday party or event, or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Franchise Inquiry Information: Your city, state or markets of interest, how you heard about us, and whether you meet our liquid capital requirement.
  • Event Information: Requested date, arcade location, event type, estimated party size, and any comments you share.
  • Job Application Information: Your application details, work history, and any résumé or other information you choose to send us when you apply for a position.
  • Transaction Information: Details about gift card purchases. Gift card payments are processed by our payment provider, Square; we do not receive or store your full payment card number.
  • Loyalty Program Information: If you join our loyalty program, your name, phone number or email address, and your visit and reward activity, which are managed through Square.

Non-Personal Data

We may also collect non-personal information about you, including but not limited to:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and the pages you have visited. This information helps us to improve our website and provide a better user experience.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, such as the features you use, the pages you visit, the links and buttons you click, and how you arrived at our site.

2. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To Provide and Maintain Our Services: Including to process transactions, manage your loyalty account, and provide customer support.
  • To Respond to Your Requests: Including to answer questions, plan birthday parties and events, review franchise inquiries, and review job applications.
  • To Improve Our Services: By understanding how users interact with our website, we can make enhancements and provide a better user experience.
  • To Communicate with You: We may use your information to contact you about updates, promotions, special offers, and other information that may be of interest to you. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time (see Section 5).
  • To Personalize Your Experience: We may use information about you to tailor the content and information that we send or display to you, and to otherwise personalize your experiences while using the website.
  • For Marketing and Promotional Purposes: We may use your information to develop and provide promotional and marketing materials and opportunities that may be of interest to you, and to measure how well our advertising works.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: We may use your information as necessary to comply with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations. Your information may be disclosed as follows:

  • With Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf and require access to such information to do that work. These include HubSpot (website forms, customer relationship management, and email), Square (gift cards, payments, and our loyalty program), Netlify (website hosting), and Google (Google Analytics).
  • With Franchisees: Some Fantasy Claw Arcade locations are independently owned and operated by franchisees. If you contact us about, plan an event at, or apply for a job at a franchisee-operated location, we may share your information with that franchisee so they can respond.
  • For Business Transfers: We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.
  • With Affiliates: We may share your information with our affiliates, in which case we will require those affiliates to honor this Privacy Policy.
  • For Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information where we are legally required to do so in order to comply with applicable law, governmental requests, judicial proceedings, court orders, or legal process.
  • To Protect Rights and Interests: We may disclose your information to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding potential violations of our policies, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person, illegal activities, or as evidence in litigation in which we are involved.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • Right to Deletion: You can request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions, such as information we must keep to comply with legal obligations.
  • Right to Opt Out of Marketing: You can unsubscribe from our marketing emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by contacting us.
  • Right to Object or Restrict Processing: You can ask us to stop or limit certain uses of your personal data.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 10. We may need to verify your identity before we respond, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the HubSpot tracking code and Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site, remember your preferences, and measure the results of our marketing. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; some parts of the site may not work properly if you do. Our website does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party websites and services, such as Square, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Yelp, and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and change the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the website after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
