Fantasy Claw Arcade Featured on the Franchise QB Podcast with Mike Halpern

Elliot Cole · Dec 15, 2025 · 1 min read · Franchise QB Podcast

Fantasy Claw Arcade recently hit the podcast spotlight to talk franchising.

Elliot Cole, our Franchising GM, joined Mike Halpern on the Franchise QB Podcast, a show dedicated to interviewing franchisors, franchisees, consultants, and other industry insiders to unpack the opportunities happening across the franchise world. Mike is a 20+ year franchise consultant with a talent for asking the right questions and highlighting brands that are doing franchising the right way.

The episode dives into how Fantasy Claw Arcade went from a spark of an idea to a fast-growing experiential retail franchise, including how we think about operations, franchisee support, training, and the nationwide rollout already underway.

If you're curious about bringing Fantasy Claw Arcade to your market, following our expansion journey, or simply interested in modern franchise models, this conversation is a great place to start.
