Serena Neel x Fantasy Claw Arcade: Play Her Machines in Las Vegas

Elliot Cole · Feb 18, 2026 · 3 min read

Some collaborations live on a screen. This one lives in the arcade, where you can walk up, grab a few plays, and be part of it in real life.

We’re excited to announce our newest partnership: Serena Neel x Fantasy Claw Arcade, now live at Fantasy Claw Arcade at Fashion Show Mall in Las Vegas. Just one day after her first video about it, the internet did its thing: millions of views.

If you know Serena, you know the vibe. She’s built a massive following by turning everyday moments into wildly entertaining content. Fun, fast, and made for sharing. And the audience is very real: Serena has about 7.9M TikTok followers, which means when she gets excited about something, people show up.

Now that energy is inside our arcade.

What’s the collaboration?

Serena has her own machines inside Fantasy Claw Arcade, stocked with a prize mix she personally curated. It’s not generic filler. It’s a lineup with Serena’s taste all over it, including hand-painted Crocs and beauty goodies like lashes and lip products.

This is a limited-time drop designed for one thing: turning a normal outing into a high-energy experience that feels like stepping into the world you’ve been watching online, except now you’re the one controlling the claw.

Why customers should visit

Fantasy Claw Arcade is built to feel like a destination: bright, high-energy, and packed with machines that keep the excitement going. The Serena machines add an extra “I have to try this” factor because they’re tied to a creator people already love, and they’re filled with prizes that feel current and worth the play.

Whether you’re:

  • visiting Vegas and want a memorable stop at the mall,
  • a local looking for a fun hangout,
  • or a Serena fan who wants to play her machines in real life…

…this is the move.

Bring friends. Bring family. Bring your competitive streak. And have your camera ready, because this collab was made for reaction videos.

Share your reaction video with Serena via the QR code on the machines

Here’s the fun part: Serena put a QR code on her machines so you can share your reaction video with her directly.

Film your moment (your setup, your grab, your celebration) and scan the QR code on the machine to send it in. If she’s featuring fan reactions in upcoming content, that’s your shot to pop up in one of her videos.

Simple playbook:

  1. Play Serena’s machine
  2. Record your reaction
  3. Scan the QR code on the machine
  4. Send it in

Short, punchy videos work best. Big reactions are encouraged. (Tasteful bragging is allowed.)

Why franchise prospects should pay attention

If you’re reading this as a potential franchise partner, here’s the bigger takeaway:

This collab is a preview of the brand engine we’re building.

The strongest modern retail concepts create reasons to visit. Moments that feel new, social, and easy to share. Creator partnerships, limited-time drops, and content-friendly experiences drive attention, and attention drives traffic.

Fantasy Claw Arcade is designed around that reality.

We’re building a franchise system that combines:

  • a highly shareable customer experience,
  • a retail model built for repeat visits,
  • and marketing that connects with how customers actually behave right now.

In other words: this isn’t “an arcade.” It’s a modern, scalable entertainment retail concept.

Come play the Serena machines in Las Vegas

If you’re in Las Vegas, stop by Fantasy Claw Arcade at Fashion Show Mall and find the Serena Neel machines. Give them a shot. Win something fun. Send your reaction to Serena.
