Fantasy Claw Arcade Featured in CoStar: Why Claw Arcade Franchises Are Gaining Momentum

Elliot Cole · Jul 23, 2026 · 2 min read · CoStar

Read the full CoStar article: Claw arcades snatch up retail space as U.S. popularity grows

Fantasy Claw Arcade was recently featured in a national CoStar News article about the rise of claw machine arcades in U.S. retail. For anyone researching a claw arcade franchise or watching the growth of location-based entertainment, that coverage reflects a bigger market trend: claw arcades are becoming a more established part of modern retail.

In the article, CoStar examines why claw arcade concepts are expanding into malls and lifestyle centers across the country. The story highlights the growing role of experiential retail and includes insight from Fantasy Claw Arcade Franchise Development Director Elliot Cole on where the brand is headed next.

Why the Claw Arcade Franchise Category Is Growing

One of the clearest themes in the article is that landlords are placing greater value on entertainment concepts that drive traffic and activate space. Claw arcades fit that need well. They are visually engaging, easy for guests to understand, and well suited for retail environments that benefit from repeat visits and high walk-by appeal.

The article also points to another advantage: claw arcades can often work in smaller retail spaces with relatively simple build-out requirements and manageable operating needs. That combination makes the format attractive in a market where flexibility matters.

For prospective operators, that is part of what makes the claw machine arcade franchise category worth watching. A concept that fits cleanly into existing retail environments has a different growth profile than one that requires a large footprint or highly complex operations.

Fantasy Claw Arcade’s Expansion Story

Fantasy Claw Arcade’s inclusion in the CoStar article reflects the momentum behind the brand’s growth. Cole shared the company’s long-term ambition clearly:

“We want to be the dominant name in claw arcade spaces. We want to ramp up.”

That vision is supported by a format designed for both guest appeal and scalability. Fantasy Claw Arcade has built its locations around immersive presentation, flexible merchandising, and strong placement in high-traffic retail centers.

The article also highlights one of the category’s biggest strengths: the ability to adjust prize selection based on local traffic and customer preferences. That flexibility allows each location to serve its market while still operating within a consistent brand model.

With locations in Las Vegas, Honolulu, and Easton Town Center in Columbus, Ohio, Fantasy Claw Arcade continues to expand in retail environments that reflect strong visibility and consumer demand.

What This Means for Prospective Franchisees

For readers exploring a claw arcade business opportunity, national coverage like this matters because it offers independent validation. It shows that the category is not being discussed only by operators. Commercial real estate firms, national retail publications, and major landlords are all paying attention to the same trend: experiential concepts with efficient footprints and strong consumer appeal are becoming more relevant in today’s retail mix.

That is part of why Fantasy Claw Arcade’s growth story matters. The brand is building in a category with increasing visibility, adaptable operations, and expanding retail interest.
