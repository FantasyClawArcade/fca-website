Fantasy Claw Arcade Is Now Open at AREA15

Elliot Cole · May 15, 2026 · 2 min read

Fantasy Claw Arcade is now open at AREA15 in Las Vegas, bringing the brand’s prize redemption arcade experience to one of the city’s most talked-about entertainment destinations.

Located inside AREA15, the new Fantasy Claw Arcade gives visitors a new way to experience arcade entertainment in Las Vegas. Known for combining strong visual appeal with a fast-paced guest experience, Fantasy Claw Arcade continues to grow in locations where immersive entertainment, high foot traffic, and destination appeal come together.

The AREA15 location is a strong fit for the brand. As an entertainment complex known for immersive attractions, nightlife, art, and interactive experiences, AREA15 attracts the kind of audience that is already looking for something memorable. Fantasy Claw Arcade adds to that mix with more than 80 prize redemption machines and a full bar, creating an experience designed for both casual visitors and groups looking for something fun to do in Las Vegas.

This opening is more than a new store launch. It is another example of Fantasy Claw Arcade’s focus on expanding into high-performing entertainment venues where the concept naturally fits the environment. The brand’s growth strategy is centered on strong locations, simple operations, and an arcade model that is built to scale while delivering a consistent guest experience.

For guests searching for an arcade at AREA15, a claw machine arcade in Las Vegas, or a new entertainment option near the Las Vegas Strip, the new location offers a clear addition to the city’s growing mix of experiential attractions. It also strengthens Fantasy Claw Arcade’s position as a modern arcade concept built for today’s entertainment customer.

As Fantasy Claw Arcade continues expanding, the AREA15 opening reflects the brand’s broader momentum and its ability to perform in premium, high-visibility venues. It is a meaningful addition to the company’s Las Vegas presence and an important step in its continued growth.

Visitors to AREA15 can now experience Fantasy Claw Arcade as part of one of the most distinctive entertainment destinations in Las Vegas.
