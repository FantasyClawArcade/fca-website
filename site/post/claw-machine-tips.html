5 Claw Machine Tips to Make the Most of Your Fantasy Claw Arcade Visit

Elliot Cole · Aug 5, 2026 · 4 min read

A visit to Fantasy Claw Arcade is about more than choosing a machine and pressing a button. Observation, timing, positioning, and a thoughtful approach can all help guests make more informed choices and enjoy every play.

To build this list, we asked Fantasy Claw Arcade team members what they see making the biggest difference on the arcade floor. These claw machine tips reflect the questions they answer, the playing habits they observe, and the practical advice they share with guests every day.

1. Walk the Arcade Before Choosing Your First Machine

It can be tempting to start with the first prize that catches your eye, but taking a quick walk through the arcade is worth the extra minute.

Different machines may feature different prize sizes, claw styles, and game formats. Some prizes may be easier to reach than others, while certain machines may simply suit your playing style better.

Be sure to visit the trade-up counter during your first walk through the space. Seeing the available prizes can help you decide what kind of experience you want to have before you begin playing.

You may arrive focused on one plush and discover another prize, machine, or goal you did not expect to find.

2. Decide on Your Strategy

Once you have explored the arcade, think about what you would like to accomplish during your visit.

Some guests want to win as many eligible prizes as possible and trade them toward something specific at the trade-up counter. Most plush and prizes qualify, although items such as food and small keychains do not. A team member at the counter can explain which prizes are eligible.

Other guests may have their eye on a particular plush inside one machine and want to focus their plays there. You may also decide to build a coordinated collection, such as a lineup of plush for a bookshelf, desk, game room, or another favorite space at home.

There is no single right way to play. The best strategy is the one that makes the visit most enjoyable for you.

Choosing a goal before you start can help you decide which machines to prioritize, when to keep trying, and when to explore a different part of the arcade. Your strategy can also change as you discover new prizes during your visit.

3. Look for Prizes That Are Primed for Picking

Before you play, take a moment to look at how the prizes are positioned.

A prize near the top of the pile, with open space around it, may give the claw a cleaner opportunity to close. A prize pressed against the glass, buried beneath other items, or tightly packed into a corner may be more difficult to reach.

At Fantasy Claw Arcade, we sometimes describe well-positioned prizes as being "primed for picking." That does not mean a win is guaranteed. It simply means the prize appears accessible enough for a clean, well-aimed attempt.

Once you have selected a prize, check the claw's position from more than one angle when the machine layout allows it. A claw may look centered from the front but appear slightly off from the side. Think from left to right and from front to back before making the drop.

A few seconds of observation can help you make a more deliberate play.

4. Pay Attention to What Happens After Each Play

Every attempt gives you information.

Even when a prize does not reach the chute, it may shift, rotate, or move into a more accessible position. Watch closely and use the result to decide what to do next.

You may want to adjust your aim and try again. You may see that the prize is now better positioned. Or you may decide that another machine is a better fit for the strategy you chose.

The goal is not to keep playing the same machine without thinking. It is to use each attempt to make a more informed decision about the next one.

5. Ask the Team for Help With a Prize

When a prize you want is buried, tightly packed, or difficult to reach, ask a team member whether the prize area can be fluffed or refreshed. When appropriate, Fantasy Claw Arcade team members may be able to open a machine and rearrange the surrounding prizes so the plush and prizes are properly presented and ready for play. This can be especially helpful when you have your eye on a specific item but it is wedged beneath other prizes or pushed into a difficult position.

The team can also help explain unfamiliar machines, point out different game formats, answer questions about the trade-up process, or help you locate a particular type of prize.

Do not hesitate to speak up! Our staff are there to make the experience easier to navigate and more enjoyable from your first play to your last.

Make the Visit Part of the Fun

The best Fantasy Claw Arcade visits are not only about the final prize. They are also about exploring the space, setting a goal, comparing machines, playing alongside friends and family, and enjoying the focus and anticipation of each attempt.

Take your time. Try different games. Build a collection, work toward a trade-up prize, or focus on the plush that first caught your attention. Celebrate the wins, learn from each play, and ask the team whenever you need assistance.

Ready to put these tips to the test? Find your nearest Fantasy Claw Arcade and start exploring.
