Our Story

Driven by our childhood love for arcades, three entrepreneurs banded together to bring more fun and excitement to the world through Japanese-inspired claw arcades. Welcome to Fantasy Claw Arcade, where nostalgia meets innovation, and every visit is a journey into a world of thrilling games and unique experiences.

Our story begins with a shared passion for the arcade games that defined our youth. We wanted to recreate that joy and excitement for a new generation, blending the best of traditional claw machines with cutting-edge technology and innovative designs. The result is Fantasy Claw Arcade, a place where skill-based gaming meets experiential retail, creating a truly unique experience for our customers.

Las Vegas: The Fun Capital of the USA

What better place to launch our dream than Las Vegas, the entertainment capital of the world? Our first location opened its doors in the bustling Fashion Show Mall on the iconic Las Vegas Strip. This vibrant, high-energy environment is the perfect backdrop for our exciting and immersive arcade experience. As you walk through our doors, you'll be transported into a world of fun, where every claw machine is a new adventure waiting to be conquered.

A Unique Gaming Experience

At Fantasy Claw Arcade, we are constantly pushing the envelope to enhance the claw experience. Our arcades feature a wide variety of claw machines, each offering different challenges and rewards. From plush toys and collectibles to tech gadgets and exclusive prizes, there's something for everyone.
