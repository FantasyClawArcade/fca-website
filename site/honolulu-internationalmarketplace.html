What to expect

Fantasy Claw Arcade brings the fun to paradise. Find 60+ prize games in the heart of Waikīkī, then line up the claw, time your drop and go for plush, collectibles and high-value prizes.

Stop by the trade-up counter for collectible upgrades. Great for families, date nights and groups.

Finding us

We're in Queen's Court on Level 1 of International Market Place.

The parking garage entrance is at Kūhiō Ave and Walina St.

Parties and groups

Birthday parties and private events are welcome here. There's no room for tables and chairs at this location, so events here are all about the games. You can also rent out the whole arcade for a private party.
