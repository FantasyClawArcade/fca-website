What to expect

Our flagship arcade on the Las Vegas Strip. Rows of Japanese-inspired claw machines, from mini claws to full-size digital cranes, each stocked with prizes to go for.

Plush of every size, nostalgic collectibles and the latest gadgets fill the machines, with new prizes arriving regularly. It's a fun stop for friends, families, date nights and visitors of all ages.

Finding us

Head to the second floor of Fashion Show Mall. We're right next to Dillard's.

Parking is free. Park on the second floor of the garage between Dick's Sporting Goods and Dillard's, near Spring Mountain Rd and Mel Torme Way.

Parties and groups

Birthday parties, group outings and private events are available here, with room for tables, chairs, food and drinks. Full-arcade rentals are available too.
