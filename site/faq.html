Visiting and playing

What is Fantasy Claw Arcade?

Fantasy Claw Arcade is an exciting arcade experience inspired by Japanese claw arcades, with a wide variety of claw machines and fun, unique prizes at our locations.

How much does it cost to play?

It depends on the token package you choose. The bigger the package, the lower the price per token. Ask at the counter for current packages.

How do tokens work?

Buy a token package at the counter, then use your tokens on any machine in the arcade. Each machine shows how many tokens a play costs. Our tokens are physical, so hang on to any you don’t use: you can save them for a future visit, and they work at every Fantasy Claw Arcade location.

How much does one play cost?

It depends on the machine. Most machines take 1 to 4 tokens per play, depending on the prize. The token cost is posted on every machine.

Any tips for winning a claw machine?

Take a moment before you play. Look for a prize sitting on top of the pile rather than buried, line up the claw from the front and the side, and aim for the prize’s center of weight. Watch someone else play to see how that machine grabs. If you’re unsure, ask our team. They know the machines well.

What can I win?

Trending plush, collectibles, trading cards (including Pokémon cards), figurines, snacks and seasonal prizes. Some stores carry local favorites, like Ohio State merchandise at Easton. We bring in new prizes regularly, so there’s something new on every visit.

What is the trade-up counter?

Trade in your winnings for something bigger or better at any of our locations. Each plush counts toward a prize tier: trade in 5 plush for a prize on the 5-point tier, 10 for the 10-point tier, 15 for the 15-point tier, or 20 for the 20-point tier.

What happens if a prize drops or a machine malfunctions?

Let a team member know. We’ll always make it right, whether that means a free play or simply handing you the prize.

Can I get a refund on tokens?

If you haven’t used any of your tokens yet, a manager can help you with a refund.

What kinds of machines do you have?

Traditional cranes, multi-player claws, slim and mini claws, digital claws, Cut-2-Win, UFO catchers, the Dragon’s Wheel, Wizard’s Vault Keymasters and the Claw King, a claw machine with ten claws at once. The mix varies by location. See Our Claws.

Is Fantasy Claw Arcade for kids or adults?

Both. Families are a big part of our crowd, and plenty of teens, collectors and adults play for prizes they want for themselves.

Is there an age requirement? Do kids need an adult?

If you can work the controls, you can play. We don’t have a set age limit, but kids should be accompanied by an adult.

Do I need a reservation to play?

No. Walk in any time during open hours. Reservations are only needed for parties and private events.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, card and mobile pay. We use Square, so most modern payment methods are accepted.

Do you give out a bag for my prizes?

Yes. Every token package comes with one of our branded clear backpacks, free, so you have somewhere to carry your prizes.

How long should I plan to spend?

As long as you like. Some guests stop in for a few minutes between stores, and others stay an hour or more working toward a trade-up prize.

Where are your arcades located?

Where is the Columbus arcade?

Are you opening a location near me?

Parties and events

Can I book a birthday party or private event?

Yes. Every location hosts birthday parties and private events, and every location can be rented out for private parties. Plan your event.

Do you have tables and room for food?

Fashion Show Mall and AREA15 have room for tables and chairs, and we can help coordinate food and drinks. Our other locations host events without seating.

What’s included in a party package?

Every party lasts 2 hours and includes:

  • A Party Host who sets up, runs the party and cleans up (two hosts for bigger groups)
  • Large pizzas sized to your group, and one drink per guest
  • Plates, napkins and cups
  • Tokens for every kid
  • 10% off anything you and your guests buy in the store during the party

Custom party packages are available for parties big or small. Tell us what you have in mind, and we’ll build a package around it.

All locations host parties, but not every location has space to set up tables and chairs. Fashion Show Mall and AREA15 do; at our other arcades, parties are all about the games. Let us know about any food allergies when you book.

How much does a party cost?

Party pricing depends on how many kids and adults are coming. Send us your date and headcount on the event form, and we’ll send you a quote.

Can I bring my own food or cake?

We coordinate the food for your party, so you don’t have to. We don’t have a kitchen on site, so if you’d like to bring your own food or a cake, let us know what you have in mind when you book and we’ll work it out together.

How far in advance should I book?

Party dates are booked first come, first served, so earlier is better. We recommend booking at least a month ahead.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A 50% deposit by card holds your date, and the balance is due on the day of the party. The final price is based on the headcount you confirm on our call the day before.

  • 7 or more days before your party: full refund, or move to another date at no charge.
  • Less than 7 days before: no refund, but your deposit can move to a new date within 60 days, one time.
  • No-show without notice: the deposit is forfeited.
  • If we have to cancel: you choose a full refund or a free reschedule.
Can I rent the whole arcade?

Yes. Full-arcade rentals are available for birthdays, corporate events and private parties. Contact us and we’ll customize your package.

Do you host corporate events and school or group outings?

Yes. We host team outings, corporate events and groups, and we offer discounts for schools, youth groups and similar organizations. Tell us your group size and date on the event form, and our store manager will work out a package with you.

How do I book?

Fill out the form on our Birthdays & Events page with your preferred arcade, date and party size. Our team will contact you to confirm the date, send your quote and take the deposit that holds your spot.

Franchise questions

How much does a Fantasy Claw Arcade franchise cost?

The total initial investment to open a Fantasy Claw Arcade starts at $292,000 per location, inclusive of the franchise fee. The single-unit franchise fee is $40,000. Multi-unit operators pay $30,000 per unit, with veteran discounts available. The investment cost varies based on location size. We target new units to fall between 1,500-2,000 square feet. Build-out costs are specific to the condition of the existing space as well as your market. You will need a minimum of $100,000 in liquid capital and a net worth of at least $300,000 to qualify.

Is a claw arcade franchise profitable?

Financial performance data for our flagship location at Fashion Show Mall in Las Vegas is in Item 19 of our Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD), which we provide to all qualified candidates during the discovery process.

What are the ongoing fees for a Fantasy Claw Arcade franchise?

Franchisees pay a royalty of 7% of net sales for single-unit operators and 6% for multi-unit operators. There is an additional 2% marketing fund contribution based on gross sales. These fees cover ongoing operational support, weekly coaching calls at opening that move to bi-weekly once you're up and running, national marketing development, and access to preferred vendor pricing on machines, prizes, and supplies.

What kind of support does Fantasy Claw Arcade provide to franchisees?

Fantasy Claw Arcade provides end-to-end support from the moment you sign through your opening day and beyond. This includes assistance with site selection and lease negotiation, full store design and build-out coordination, machine sourcing, and prize inventory setup. Owner training covers 100-plus hours of hands-on instruction including machine servicing, guest experience protocols, and POS system management. Staff training runs 48 hours. After opening, franchisees receive weekly support calls, moving to bi-weekly once they're up and running, plus quarterly performance reviews.

How long does it take to open a Fantasy Claw Arcade franchise?

Our goal is to have you open your first location within six months of signing the Franchise Agreement, depending on lease timelines and local permitting. The franchise team assists with site selection and lease negotiation to accelerate this process. Build-out and machine installation are coordinated by the corporate team to minimize delays.

What locations work best for a Fantasy Claw Arcade?

Fantasy Claw Arcade is purpose-built for high-traffic retail environments, specifically enclosed malls, shopping centers, and tourist-oriented destinations. Current and upcoming locations are in Fashion Show Mall, Area15, and Miracle Mile Shops in Las Vegas, Ala Moana Center and International Market Place in Honolulu, and Easton Town Center in Columbus, Ohio. The brand targets markets that already support family entertainment concepts like Dave & Buster's and Chuck E. Cheese.

Who is the ideal Fantasy Claw Arcade franchisee?

Fantasy Claw Arcade is best suited for hands-on operators and experienced multi-unit leaders who are business-minded, operationally focused, and committed to delivering a strong guest experience. You do not need an arcade or entertainment background since the training program is designed to bring new operators up to speed completely. You do need to be financially qualified, operationally serious, and ready to follow an established system.

What makes Fantasy Claw Arcade different from other arcade franchises?

Fantasy Claw Arcade is a dedicated claw arcade concept, not a general arcade. New franchise locations typically house 50 to 100 machines, with approximately 90% being claw-operated prize redemption machines. The wizard-themed environment, token-based gameplay, and prize mix of trending plush, collectibles, trading cards, and snack brands are designed specifically to drive repeat visits and social media sharing. The branded clear backpacks guests use to carry prizes function as walking billboards.

Are territories still available?

Yes. Fantasy Claw Arcade is actively awarding franchise agreements across the United States. Select prime territories in major metro markets are available now. Qualified candidates are encouraged to submit their information early since territory availability is determined on a first-come, first-served basis as franchise agreements are signed.

How do I get started?

Submit your contact information using the form on our Franchising page. A member of the Fantasy Claw Arcade franchise team will schedule an introductory call to discuss the opportunity and confirm mutual fit. From there you will receive the Franchise Disclosure Document, go through a structured discovery process, and if approved, sign your Franchise Agreement. The process from first contact to signed agreement typically takes 60 to 90 days.
