What to expect

70+ prize machines on the second floor of Easton's Station Building. Line up the claw, time your drop and go for plush, collectibles and high-value prizes.

Stop by the trade-up counter for collectible upgrades. Great for families, date nights and groups.

Finding us

We're on the second floor of the Station Building.

Parking is free in the West Garage or East Garage, on either side of the Station Building between Gramercy St and Easton Station.

Parties and groups

Birthday parties and private events are welcome here. There's no room for tables and chairs at this location, so events here are all about the games. You can also rent out the whole arcade for a private party.
